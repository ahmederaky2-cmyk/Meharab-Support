Location and travel

Tap “Use my location” when you want GPS calculations. You may decline permission and enter latitude/longitude in Settings. Coordinates stay in memory and must be entered again after restarting. Manual locations are never replaced by automatic travel updates. GPS refreshes while the app is in use; background tracking is not enabled.

Qibla compass

Enable the compass, hold your phone level and keep it away from magnets and metal. A north-up diagram is a reference, not a live heading. Magnetic and browser estimates are labeled as approximate. Use a calibrated true-north compass if readings are unreliable.

Prayer times

Choose your calculation method, Asr school and high-latitude rule in Settings. For distant manual coordinates, select the destination time zone. Prayer times are astronomical estimates and can differ from your mosque’s timetable. Sunrise is a reference rather than a prayer.

Hijri calendar

The default is the calculated Saudi Umm al-Qura calendar, using your device’s local civil date. Supported dates: 20 November 1979–16 November 2077. Dates change at civil midnight, not sunset, and do not confirm an official moon sighting.

Settings provides a −2 to +2 day adjustment and custom starts for a particular Hijri month and year. An asterisk marks adjusted dates. Custom starts must keep neighboring months at 29 or 30 days; you may need to draft several neighboring starts together. “Restore Saudi default” clears both corrections and custom starts without changing prayer calculation settings.

Offline use and languages

Qibla, prayer and Hijri calculations run offline once a location is available or entered manually. Choose English, German or Arabic in Settings. Preferences are stored locally.

Hilfe auf Deutsch

Standort und Berechnungsmethode lassen sich in den Einstellungen wählen. Manuelle Koordinaten bleiben unverändert. Der berechnete Umm-al-Qura-Kalender kann lokal korrigiert werden. Bei ungenauen Kompasswerten Abstand zu Metall und Magneten halten. Für Hilfe den Support-Link verwenden.
