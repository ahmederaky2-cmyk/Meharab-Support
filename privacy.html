Pre-release policy. The production native dependency and runtime audit is not complete. App Store privacy declarations have not been finalized. This page describes the current app design and source implementation and will be reviewed against the signed release before distribution.

Who provides Meharab

Meharab is maintained by the developer using the GitHub account ahmederaky2-cmyk. Questions may be submitted through the public support tracker. Legal publisher details must match the eventual App Store listing.

Location and compass

When you choose GPS and grant foreground location permission, Meharab uses the current coordinates on your device to calculate the Qibla bearing and prayer times. You can instead enter coordinates manually. The app source does not send coordinates to external calculation services or a developer server, save GPS positions between sessions, or keep a location history. Current coordinates and heading samples are held in memory for calculations. Background location tracking is not enabled. You can revoke permission in system settings.

Your operating system controls location and sensor services and may use its own network services under the platform provider’s policies. These system services are separate from a developer-operated Meharab backend.

Local preferences

Language, prayer method, Asr school, high-latitude rule, selected display time zone, Hijri date adjustment and custom Hijri month starts are saved in local device storage. They are not uploaded by the app. The Hijri restore control clears calendar corrections. Other preferences can be changed in Settings or removed with app data, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior. Manual/GPS coordinates are not part of saved preferences.

Accounts, advertising and online services

The current app has no account system, advertising, third-party analytics, tracking SDK, remote logging, developer backend or cloud database. Prayer, Qibla and Hijri calculations use local code and bundled data. Automatic remote calendar synchronization and over-the-air updates are disabled. Development tools may contact development services; they are distinct from the standalone production app. Final native SDK behavior still requires release verification.

Retention and control

There is no developer-held location history or Meharab cloud account in the current implementation. You may stop GPS use, revoke permission, use manual coordinates, reset calendar corrections or remove the app and its local data.

This website and support

This public help site is hosted by GitHub Pages and contains no added analytics, cookies or third-party assets. Visiting the site sends ordinary web request information, such as an IP address, to GitHub under GitHub’s privacy statement. Support issues are public and may remain in repository history. Do not post personal or sensitive information. The app does not automatically submit support reports.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated if the implementation or data practices change. Contact the developer through the support tracker.
